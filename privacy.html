Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

This policy explains how the izlio app for Android TV and Google TV handles data. Baseflow Technology OÜ, Estonia ("we") is the publisher of the app and the data controller for this policy. The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to this policy.

Short version

  • We do not collect your data. The app has no account with us.
  • The app has no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your provider login. For Xtream Codes: server address, user name and password. For an M3U link: the playlist link. For an M3U file: only a reference to the file (the app does not copy the file). The app encrypts the password and the playlist link with a key in the Android Keystore.
  • The channel, movie, series and program guide lists that your provider sends.
  • Your favorites, your own lists, watch positions, guide reminders, hidden countries, groups and categories, and subtitle and audio settings (for example the last screen and language).
  • A cache of images (logos and posters) from your provider.

The app turns off Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for this data. The data stays on this device.

Signing out removes the login. Your favorites, lists and watch positions stay on the device until you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

What leaves your device

The app connects to the internet only to get content that you asked for:

  • Requests to your own provider: login check, lists, program guide, details, images and the streams you play. Your provider receives your login and your IP address. Their privacy policy applies to that data.
  • Image addresses (logos and posters) that your provider gives. The app loads them from the host in the address. That host and your provider see your IP address, like any web server.
  • If you open a trailer, the app starts the YouTube app on your device. YouTube then handles that request under its own policy.
  • If you choose an external player (for example VLC or MX Player), the app gives the stream address to that player.

The connection test in Settings contacts only your provider.

The app does not send data to us or to any other third party.

Phone login by QR code

You can type your login on your phone. The TV shows a QR code. The phone opens a short-lived page that the TV serves on your local network. The phone sends the login in encrypted form directly to the TV. The data does not go to the internet or to us. The code and its key are valid for 10 minutes; then the TV makes a new one.

Permissions

The app asks for no permission that you must approve. It uses network access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE). Android libraries in the app also declare WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED for background list updates on the device; they do not send data to us.

Content

izlio does not provide, host or sell channels, movies or streams. You are responsible for the content that you add and for your provider account.

No sale of data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We do not use it for advertising.

Children

izlio is not made for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. We collect no data from anyone. If you think a child sent us data, write to us and we delete it.

When you contact us

If you write to izlio [at] baseflow [dot] ee, we use your e-mail address and your message only to answer you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest to answer support requests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We delete support e-mails at the latest 24 months after the case is closed. We do not share them.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. GitHub can log your IP address for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability of your personal data, and you can object. The app sends no data to us, so we normally hold no data about you, except support e-mails. Write to izlio [at] baseflow [dot] ee. You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, aki.ee) or to the authority in your country.

Delete your data

Choose Sign out in the app to remove the login. To remove all data, clear the app data in Android Settings or uninstall the app. See the support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we publish the new text here and change the date above.
